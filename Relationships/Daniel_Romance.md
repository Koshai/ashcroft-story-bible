# REL-007 — Daniel and Romantic Partner

**Status:** OPEN FRAMEWORK

## Relationship Principle

The partner does not exist to complete Daniel or reward the player. She has her own life, judgments, errors, limits, and evolving relationship to Daniel's work.

## Case Integration

The relationship should be affected by choices made across many cases:

- Cancelled commitments
- Truthful or deceptive explanations
- Emotional availability
- Risk-taking
- Confidentiality
- Moral compromises
- Whether Daniel listens when challenged
- Whether the partner supports, resists, or enables him

## Positive Possibilities

- Daniel becomes more patient and emotionally grounded.
- Trust allows honest disagreement.
- The partner identifies changes in Daniel before he does.
- Both learn to distinguish privacy from deception.

## Negative Possibilities

- Daniel interrogates rather than communicates.
- The partner becomes resentful, fearful, controlling, or overly involved.
- Support turns into enabling obsession.
- Daniel uses professional secrecy to justify emotional dishonesty.
- The relationship collapses or becomes mutually damaging.

## Key Question

> When this is all over, who will Daniel be?
