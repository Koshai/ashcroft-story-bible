# REL-006 — Daniel and Mike

## Foundation

Mike is the chosen friend who knew Daniel before his professional identity formed.

## Contrast

- Daniel left Eastside Flats.
- Mike returned or remained.
- Daniel measures progress through truths uncovered.
- Mike measures life through work completed, people helped, and responsibilities accepted.

## Emotional Question

Daniel may wonder whether he would have made Mike's sacrifice if their lives were reversed.

Mike does not blame Daniel or present his own life as morally superior.

## Functional Difference From Becca

Becca focuses on consequences for people.

Mike focuses on alternative paths, assumptions, tradeoffs, and the possibility that a life can change direction without becoming a failure.
