# Mercer Family Relationships

## REL-001 — Daniel ↔ Becca

Love, friction, humor, and complementary moral lenses.

- Daniel pursues contradictions.
- Becca protects people.
- Daniel can become emotionally absent.
- Becca can become overly trusting.
- Neither is always correct.

## REL-002 — Daniel ↔ Elaine

Elaine sees through Daniel's evasions and remembers the person he was before work consumed him. Daniel may feel disconnected from the life Elaine and Becca continued living.

## REL-003 — Daniel ↔ Thomas

Daniel inherited Thomas's curiosity and respect for physical truth. He may have elevated a practical remark into a life philosophy.

## REL-004 — Becca ↔ Thomas

Becca inherited Thomas's compassion and attention to burdens people do not reveal.

## REL-005 — Elaine ↔ Thomas

A loving but imperfect marriage built through repeated choices rather than idealized perfection.

## Family Tradition — Sunday Dinner

Elaine continues cooking Sunday dinner after Thomas's death.

- Becca rarely misses it.
- Daniel often does.
- The game does not always force attendance.
- Empty chairs and missed conversations silently track Daniel's priorities.

## Core Family Principle

The Mercer family is not secretly corrupt, criminal, or tied to the conspiracy. Their narrative power comes from ordinary history, not hidden plot machinery.
