## Current Canon Summary

Ashcroft City is a fictional American industrial harbor city founded in 1884 and reinvented around finance, technology, biomedical research, education, real estate, and international shipping. Its celebrated progress hides accumulated inequality and unresolved historical compromises.

Daniel Mercer, a former APD investigative analyst, has opened a struggling private investigation office. His defining strength is identifying contradictions. His defining weakness is that he cannot let contradictions go.

Daniel's personal circle does not solve cases for him. They shape the detective capable of solving them.

The exact truth of the main conspiracy remains unresolved. Its form is now established as a distributed network that exploits fragmentation among otherwise functioning institutions. Daniel uncovers the pattern; lawful institutions must ultimately act on it.
