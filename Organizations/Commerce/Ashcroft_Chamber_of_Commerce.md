# ORG-COM-001 — Ashcroft Chamber of Commerce

**Status:** CANON FOUNDATION  
**Primary Ecosystem:** ECO-004 — Commerce

## Overview

The Ashcroft Chamber of Commerce is a voluntary nonprofit organization that supports local businesses, entrepreneurship, workforce development, civic events, and cooperation between business, government, and community institutions.

## Mission

> A stronger business community creates a stronger Ashcroft.

## History

Founded in 1954 during the city's manufacturing boom, the Chamber evolved from an industrial business association into a cross-sector civic network.

## Members

- Independent businesses
- Banks and credit unions
- Insurers
- Construction and real-estate firms
- Hospitals and healthcare providers
- Ashcroft University
- Manufacturers
- Shipping and logistics companies
- Hotels and restaurants
- Technology companies
- Professional-service firms

## Narrative and Investigation Function

The Chamber is a credible social crossroads. Public events, mentorship programs, fundraisers, and business forums allow people from different ecosystems to know one another without implying conspiracy.

The institution itself should rarely be the villain. Individual members may become relevant because of their own decisions.

## Future Character Needs

No Chamber leadership is canon yet. Leadership should be created when a case or the Game Spine requires a recurring Commerce representative.
