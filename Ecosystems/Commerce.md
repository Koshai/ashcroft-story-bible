# Commerce Ecosystem

**ID:** ECO-004

**Status:** CANON FOUNDATION

## Purpose

Commerce moves money, property, labor, and goods through Ashcroft. It includes banking, insurance, real estate, construction, shipping, logistics, manufacturing, retail, hospitality, professional services, technology, and small businesses.

## Ordinary Purpose

The ecosystem exists to provide jobs, build property, finance households and businesses, distribute goods, and support daily life.

## Historical Shape

Ashcroft moved through three broad economic periods:

1. Industrial and manufacturing expansion
2. Late-century decline and property devaluation
3. Diversified reinvention through healthcare, education, technology, finance, logistics, and redevelopment

## Investigation Relevance

Commerce naturally generates records and relationships:

- Contracts
- Ownership
- Insurance claims
- Loans
- Shipping manifests
- Payroll
- Permits
- Purchases
- Property transfers
- Professional services

These are not inherently suspicious. They become meaningful when people use ordinary systems dishonestly or when records reveal connections across cases.

## Scope Rule

Economic statistics remain approximate design guidance rather than hard canon until cases require specific figures.
