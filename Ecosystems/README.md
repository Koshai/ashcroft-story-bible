# Ashcroft City Ecosystems

**Status:** CANON

## Purpose

The ecosystem layer describes how Ashcroft functions independently of Daniel Mercer and any single case. An ecosystem is a connected set of organizations, professions, infrastructure, communities, and dependencies that performs a necessary city function.

Organizations are individual entities. Ecosystems explain how those entities interact.

## Core Principle

> Complexity should emerge from relationships between systems, not from the number of systems.

Ashcroft prioritizes depth over breadth. The goal is not to simulate every part of a metropolis, but to make a focused set of recurring institutions increasingly meaningful.

## Rules

1. Every organization belongs to one primary ecosystem.
2. An organization may interact with several ecosystems but must have one clear institutional home.
3. Every ecosystem functions whether or not Daniel is present.
4. Stories emerge from dependencies and collisions between ecosystems.
5. Existing systems should be deepened before new systems are added.
6. A new institution must strengthen the existing city rather than merely enlarge it.
7. Recurring representatives may introduce an ecosystem, but no person controls or embodies the whole system.

## ECO-001 — Government

Maintains civic administration and public infrastructure.

Potential organizations:

- City Hall
- Mayor's Office
- Planning and Zoning
- Public Works
- Licensing and Permits
- Elections Administration

Typical connections include contracts, redevelopment, permits, budgets, infrastructure, and public accountability.

## ECO-002 — Justice

Investigates, prosecutes, adjudicates, and responds to crime.

Organizations include:

- ORG-001 — Ashcroft Police Department
- District Attorney
- Courts
- Public Defender
- Probation and Corrections

Typical connections include evidence standards, witness credibility, legal procedure, convictions, appeals, and institutional limits.

## ECO-003 — Health

Protects public health, treats injury, and investigates death.

Potential organizations:

- ORG-004 — Ashcroft Medical Center
- Emergency Medical Services
- Medical Examiner
- Clinics
- Mental-health services
- Long-term care facilities

Typical connections include medical history, unidentified patients, overdose patterns, workplace injuries, capacity, and privacy.

## ECO-004 — Commerce

Moves money, property, labor, and goods through Ashcroft.

Potential organizations:

- Banks and investment firms
- Insurance companies
- Construction and real-estate companies
- Shipping and warehousing firms
- Manufacturers
- Local businesses

Typical connections include ownership, logistics, contracts, fraud, debt, property, employment, and supply chains.

## ECO-005 — Information

Creates, preserves, interprets, and distributes knowledge.

Organizations include or may include:

- ORG-005 — Ashcroft Chronicle
- Television and radio
- Independent journalists
- Libraries and archives
- ORG-003 — Ashcroft University

Typical connections include public narrative, institutional memory, research, records, leaks, misinformation, and archives.

## ECO-006 — Community

Represents Ashcroft's social fabric and everyday relationships.

Potential organizations and places:

- Schools
- Religious institutions
- Community centers
- Charities
- Sports clubs
- Neighborhood associations

Typical connections include trust, reputation, mutual aid, family history, local memory, and human consequences.

## Cross-Ecosystem Story Generation

Stories arise where responsibilities overlap:

- Government + Commerce — procurement, redevelopment, zoning, and contracts
- Justice + Health — homicide, injury, death investigation, and privacy
- Commerce + Justice — fraud, theft, labor exploitation, and financial crime
- Information + Government — public records, leaks, elections, and accountability
- Community + Health — addiction, recovery, access, and caregiving
- Information + Justice — reporting, confidentiality, and public pressure

The greater mystery should grow through repeated cross-ecosystem connections rather than through endless new factions.

## Scope Control

Ashcroft should have a limited set of important recurring characters, organizations, and locations. Familiarity creates depth. New additions must pass the question:

> Does this make Ashcroft deeper, or only bigger?
