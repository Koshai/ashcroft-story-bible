# CHAR-001 — Daniel "Danny" Mercer

**Status:** CANON FOUNDATION  
**Age:** 34  
**Occupation:** Private investigator  
**Former Occupation:** APD investigative analyst  
**Childhood Home:** Eastside Flats  
**Current Residence:** Northwood Park  
**Office:** Near the Historic Quarter

## Core Personality

- Observant
- Patient
- Quietly stubborn
- Dry sense of humor
- Highly analytical
- Prone to obsession

## Defining Strength

Daniel recognizes contradictions and connections between apparently unrelated information.

## Defining Flaw

Once something does not fit, he struggles to let it go. His persistence can become emotional withdrawal, tunnel vision, and self-destruction.

## Foundational Philosophy

> People can lie. Things don't.

The phrase comes from Thomas Mercer. Its mundane original context may involve a defective used Buick, making Daniel's near-scriptural interpretation both meaningful and human.

## Career Background

Daniel worked as an APD investigative analyst specializing in:

- Phone records
- CCTV analysis
- Financial analysis
- Digital evidence
- Cross-case connections

He was not a sworn detective. This background naturally supports gameplay centered on records, evidence, timelines, and contradictions.

## Marcus Vale Incident

Three years before the game, warehouse employee Marcus Vale disappeared. The official account claimed Marcus stole approximately $47,000 and fled. Daniel found irregular shipping access, questionable cargo activity, and suspicious corporate transactions.

His superior told him:

> That's not our case.

Daniel continued. He was reassigned, had access restricted, and eventually left APD.

## Starting Condition

Daniel has operated his private office for approximately three months. He has few clients, unpaid bills, and a growing fear that leaving APD may have destroyed his career.

## Starting Worldview

Daniel broadly believes that uncovering truth enables better decisions. The story will repeatedly challenge the assumption that truth automatically produces justice or healing.

## Character Arc

### Beginning — Observer

- Detached
- Analytical
- Convinced that sufficient evidence resolves uncertainty

### Middle — Investigator / Obsession

- Increasingly capable
- Increasingly isolated
- Starts treating people as components of cases
- Relationships begin reflecting the cost of his pursuit

### Turning Point — Home

Daniel returns to his roots and rediscovers the strengths his family gave him. He learns to combine evidence with history, empathy, perspective, moral awareness, and reversed assumptions.

### Late — Cost / Detective

Daniel must determine whether becoming a great detective requires losing the person he was trying to protect.

## Failure Principle

Daniel may accuse the wrong person, miss evidence, damage relationships, or leave cases unresolved. Mistakes become part of the story rather than automatic game-over screens.
