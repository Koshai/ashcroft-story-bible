# CHAR-003 — Elaine Margaret Mercer

**Status:** CANON FOUNDATION  
**Age:** Approximately 62  
**Occupation:** Longtime school administrative secretary; retirement status to confirm  
**Residence:** Eastside Flats  
**Marital Status:** Widow

## Core Concept

Elaine remembers people and their histories. Daniel remembers information. Elaine remembers what happened before the information existed.

## Personality

- Practical
- Social
- Perceptive
- Sarcastic when needed
- Protective without being melodramatic
- Extremely difficult for Daniel to deceive

## Narrative Function

**Memory, life history, and context.**

Elaine teaches Daniel to ask:

> Who was this person before the case began?

## Relationship With Daniel

Elaine understands that Daniel was always going to investigate. Her fear is not simply that he will be harmed. She fears he will become emotionally unreachable.

Thomas sometimes disappeared into work, but he returned home. Elaine is no longer certain Daniel knows how to return.

## Relationship With Becca

Elaine and Becca share daily life in Eastside Flats. Daniel can feel like an outsider because the community continued while he was elsewhere.

## Marriage With Thomas

**CANON:** Loving but imperfect.

They argued about money, work hours, parenting, and exhaustion. They repeatedly chose each other. There is no required affair, hidden family, or conspiracy secret.

## Social Memory

Decades in the school system gave Elaine deep knowledge of families, teachers, police officers, lawyers, doctors, business owners, and former students. This does not make her an informant; it makes her a witness to Ashcroft's social history.

## Mercer House

The Mercer home should become a recurring emotional location:

- Familiar wallpaper
- Family photographs
- Thomas's workshop
- Sunday meals
- Ordinary household sounds
- An atmosphere distinct from investigation spaces

## The Attic Box — PROPOSED

An ordinary cardboard box contains notes, report cards, photographs, bills, cards, and keepsakes. It does not hide a conspiracy clue. Its value is emotional truth.
