# CHAR-005 — Michael "Mike" Sullivan

**Status:** CANON DIRECTION / DETAILS OPEN  
**Age:** Approximately 35  
**Occupation:** Auto mechanic and owner of Sullivan's Garage  
**Residence:** Eastside Flats

## Core Concept

Mike knew Daniel before anyone called him Detective Mercer. He is not impressed by Daniel's professional identity and remembers the child beneath it.

## Childhood

Daniel and Mike met around age eight. They rode bicycles, explored Greywater Basin, got into harmless trouble, and spent time around Thomas repairing machines.

## Changed Dream

As a young man, Mike had a larger technical or racing-related ambition. Possibilities include:

- Professional racing
- Mechanical engineering
- Racing development program

Life redirected him. A family illness, financial pressure, or responsibility for the garage brought him home temporarily, and temporary became permanent.

Exact dream and catalyst remain OPEN.

## Crucial Rule

Mike is not secretly miserable. He genuinely values the garage and the life he built, while occasionally wondering what another path might have looked like.

## Narrative Function

**Perspective, alternative paths, reversed assumptions, and meaningful compromise.**

Mike teaches Daniel to ask:

> What if the original assumption is backward?

He also embodies the question:

> What makes a meaningful life?

## Effect on Daniel

Mike gave up or reshaped a dream for people. Daniel often gives up people for the pursuit of truth.

This creates uncertainty rather than a simple moral answer. Daniel wonders whether he would have returned for family if their circumstances were reversed.

## Gameplay and Story Use

Mike does not solve cases. Conversations with him may prompt Daniel to:

- Reverse the assumed motive
- Consider a path a suspect could have taken but rejected
- Examine sacrifice versus ambition
- Recognize that not every choice has one correct answer
- Step away from obsessive thought and return with a clearer mind
