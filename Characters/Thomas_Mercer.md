# CHAR-004 — Thomas Mercer

**Status:** PROPOSED FOUNDATION  
**Occupation:** Industrial maintenance worker  
**Status:** Deceased

## Core Concept

Thomas shaped both children in different ways.

- Daniel inherited curiosity, observation, and a respect for physical evidence.
- Becca inherited compassion, loyalty, and attention to invisible burdens.

## Known Sayings

> People can lie. Things don't.

> Everybody's carrying something you can't see.

The second line remains PROPOSED.

## Marriage

Thomas and Elaine had a loving but imperfect marriage. They faced financial stress, exhausting work schedules, and ordinary disagreements.

## Portrayal Rule

Thomas must not become a flawless dead father whose wisdom explains everything. He was a working-class man who made mistakes, worried about money, argued, sometimes worked too much, and was not always emotionally available.

## Objective Truth vs. Memory

Daniel remembers fixing objects and asking how things worked.

Becca remembers school events, neighborly kindness, and second chances.

Elaine remembers the complete adult: strengths, flaws, frustrations, humor, and compromises.

## Death

Current preference: Thomas's death remains ordinary and unrelated to the main conspiracy.

Exact cause, date, and circumstances remain OPEN.
