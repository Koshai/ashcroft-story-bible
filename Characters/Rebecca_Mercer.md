# CHAR-002 — Rebecca "Becca" Mercer

**Status:** CANON FOUNDATION  
**Age:** 30  
**Occupation:** Public-school teacher  
**Workplace:** Eastside Flats  
**Residence:** Eastside Flats

## Core Concept

Daniel left Eastside Flats. Becca stayed.

Daniel understands Ashcroft through records, systems, and contradictions. Becca understands it through people, families, children, and consequences.

## Personality

- Warm but not endlessly patient
- Socially perceptive
- Practical
- Quick-witted
- Independent
- Protective of her students
- Stubborn

## Narrative Function

**Compassion and human consequences.**

Becca does not solve Daniel's cases. She changes the questions he asks.

Daniel asks:

> Did this person do it?

Becca asks:

> What happens to everyone else if you're right?

## Relationship With Daniel

They love each other deeply, but Daniel disappears into work, misses calls, cancels plans, and returns when his life is collapsing.

Potential line:

> You don't live here anymore, Danny. You investigate here.

## Relationship With Thomas

Thomas had a different but equally meaningful relationship with each child.

- Daniel inherited Thomas's curiosity.
- Becca inherited Thomas's compassion.

Neither child fully understood every side of him. Each may sometimes believe the other knew him better.

## Shared Mercer Trait

Daniel refuses to abandon a question. Becca refuses to abandon a person.

## Gameplay Role

Becca may cause Daniel and the player to consider:

- Victims' families
- Children of suspects
- Community consequences
- Whether justice can create additional harm
- Whether compassion is illuminating or dangerously naive

She is not always correct. Her empathy can become misplaced trust or denial.
