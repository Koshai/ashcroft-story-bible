# Founding Discussion Summary

## Creative Direction

The project began as a narrative-heavy detective game designed around genuine deduction rather than hidden-object puzzles or scripted answer selection. The detective board should become a central mechanic, and the story should be strong enough to stand alone as a novel or television drama.

The user requested a collaborative writers' room approach in which major creative decisions are presented as options and canonized only after explicit approval.

## Core Structural Insight

The game has two intertwined arcs:

1. Daniel investigates crimes and the history of Ashcroft.
2. Daniel is shaped by family, friendship, romance, mistakes, and the consequences of his work.

The personal circle should not function as an information network. Instead, each relationship gives Daniel a different lens:

- Thomas — What can be proven?
- Elaine — Who was this person before the event?
- Becca — Who is affected if Daniel is right?
- Mike — What if Daniel's original assumption is reversed?
- Romantic Partner — Who is Daniel becoming, and can he still trust and be trusted?

## Major Approved Choices

- City: Ashcroft City
- Sister: Rebecca Mercer, public-school teacher in Eastside Flats
- Thomas's relationship with children: different but equally meaningful
- Elaine and Thomas's marriage: loving but imperfect
- Best friend: Mike Sullivan, whose original dream changed when life redirected him toward the family garage
- Romance direction: slow-burn relationship outside ordinary case roles, but capable of affecting multiple cases and Daniel's approach depending on player choices

## Home Chapter

A dedicated chapter or short sequence should return Daniel to his roots. It may involve Thomas's workshop, family photographs, handwritten notes, report cards, old keepsakes, and ordinary family history.

The chapter should not reveal a conspiracy clue. Its purpose is to restore Daniel's inner strength and reshape how he approaches future investigations:

- Holistic thinking
- Reversed assumptions
- Moral and ethical awareness
- Awareness of missing context
- Motivation after failure
- Recognition of human consequences
- Return to first principles

The Home chapter is not an isolated sentimental interlude. It should have a lasting effect on Daniel's detective philosophy.

## Risk Identified

The project is accumulating many strong ideas. To prevent drift, every future addition must strengthen an existing pillar and have a unique narrative function.
