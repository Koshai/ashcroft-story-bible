# Discarded or Unselected Ideas

## Rebecca Mercer Alternatives

- Public defender
- Architect / urban planner
- Emergency-room nurse

Teacher was selected because it anchors her in Eastside Flats and prevents the Mercer family from becoming an investigative resource network.

## Thomas and Elaine Marriage Alternatives

- Marriage with an unspoken fracture
- Separation before Thomas's death

A loving but imperfect marriage was selected to avoid unnecessary family-secret escalation.

## Mike Sullivan Alternatives

- Fully stable family life as Daniel's road-not-taken
- Unmarried community anchor

The changed-dream direction was selected because it creates a richer contrast involving sacrifice, compromise, and alternative definitions of success.

## Romance Alternatives

- Existing relationship at game start
- Justice-system professional
- Romance beginning directly through a case

Slow-burn romance outside normal case functions was preferred, while allowing the relationship to interact with multiple cases through consequences.
