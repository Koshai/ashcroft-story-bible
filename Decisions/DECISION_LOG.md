# Decision Log

## DEC-001 — City Concept

**Chosen:** Ashcroft City  
**Reason:** Supports industrial history, modern finance and technology, local and international crime, political corruption, and a grounded neo-noir tone.

## DEC-002 — Protagonist Career

**Chosen:** Former APD investigative analyst, not former detective  
**Reason:** Naturally supports records, CCTV, phone, financial, and cross-case analysis while preserving tension with sworn investigators.

## DEC-003 — Daniel's Sister

**Options:** Public defender, architect, nurse, teacher  
**Chosen:** Teacher in Eastside Flats  
**Reason:** Grounds her in community life and prevents her from functioning as a convenient legal, medical, or investigative resource.

## DEC-004 — Thomas's Relationship With His Children

**Options:** Closer to Daniel, closer to Becca, different relationship with each  
**Chosen:** Different relationship with each  
**Reason:** Daniel inherits curiosity; Becca inherits compassion. Neither owns the complete memory of Thomas.

## DEC-005 — Elaine and Thomas's Marriage

**Options:** Loving but imperfect, hidden fracture, separated  
**Chosen:** Loving but imperfect  
**Reason:** Provides emotional realism without making every family relationship depend on a dramatic secret.

## DEC-006 — Best Friend Direction

**Options:** Stable family life, changed dream, community anchor  
**Chosen:** Changed dream  
**Reason:** Mike's reshaped ambition creates a meaningful contrast with Daniel's sacrifices and asks what constitutes a successful life.

## DEC-007 — Supporting-Circle Function

**Chosen:** Personal relationships shape Daniel's thinking but do not solve cases  
**Reason:** Preserves character agency and integrates personal life with detective growth.

## DEC-008 — Home Chapter

**Chosen:** Dedicated roots-focused chapter or sequence  
**Reason:** Gives Daniel motivation, emotional restoration, and a more holistic investigative philosophy without relying on a conspiracy revelation.

## DEC-009 — Romance Direction

**Options:** Existing relationship, slow-burn outside cases, justice-system partner, case-based romance  
**Chosen:** Slow-burn outside ordinary case roles  
**Reason:** Keeps personal life independent while allowing choices across multiple cases to shape trust, vulnerability, and Daniel's evolution.

## DEC-010 — Project Organization

**Chosen:** Git-ready Markdown Story Bible with immutable canon, living canon, sandbox, IDs, decision logs, and changelog  
**Reason:** Prevents continuity drift as the cast, cases, and world expand.

## DEC-011 — APD Moral Foundation

**Chosen:** APD is a fundamentally legitimate and generally ethical institution.  
**Reason:** The story explores institutional limits and fragmented responsibility rather than reducing professional conflict to universal police corruption.

## DEC-012 — APD Structure

**Options:** Centralized citywide department; semi-independent precinct cultures  
**Chosen:** Centralized citywide department  
**Reason:** Keeps the cast and world focused, supports recurring professional relationships, and allows investigations to remain interconnected across districts.

## DEC-013 — Intentional Scope

**Chosen:** New elements must deepen the existing world before expanding it.  
**Reason:** Complexity should emerge from relationships rather than from an uncontrolled number of characters, locations, factions, or systems.

## DEC-014 — Greater Mystery Structure

**Chosen:** Distributed network rather than a singular secret organization  
**Reason:** Allows ordinary crimes, institutions, and transactions to form a larger pattern without requiring one mastermind or total institutional control.

## DEC-015 — APD Role in the Greater Mystery

**Chosen:** APD is initially limited by scope, later becomes an institutional ally.  
**Reason:** Daniel's role is to reveal the cross-case pattern; arrests, prosecution, and public justice remain collective civic functions.

## DEC-016 — City Ecosystem Framework

**Chosen:** Six core ecosystems — Government, Justice, Health, Commerce, Information, and Community  
**Reason:** Provides a compact structure for connecting organizations and generating stories while preserving depth-over-breadth scope control.

## DEC-017 — Detective Philosophy Record

**Chosen:** Track Daniel's evolving principles separately from gameplay perks.  
**Reason:** Daniel grows through perspective, relationships, mistakes, and reflection rather than conventional upgrades.
