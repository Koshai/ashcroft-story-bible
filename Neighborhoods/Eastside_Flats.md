# LOC-014 — Eastside Flats

**Status:** CANON FOUNDATION

## Identity

Eastside Flats is a working- and middle-class neighborhood shaped by industrial decline, generational continuity, local businesses, schools, tradespeople, and strong informal community knowledge.

It is Daniel and Becca Mercer's childhood home and the current home of Elaine and Becca. Sullivan's Garage operates here.
