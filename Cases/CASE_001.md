# CASE-001 — Opening Missing-Property Case

**Status:** CANON FOUNDATION / DETAILS OPEN

## Opening

Rain. Bills. No meaningful clients.

A woman enters Daniel's small office with an apparently minor missing-property problem.

## Narrative Function

- Introduce investigation mechanics
- Establish Daniel's financial and emotional starting point
- Demonstrate that small cases can reveal meaningful human truths
- Avoid immediately announcing the main conspiracy

## Open Questions

- What property is missing?
- Why does it matter emotionally?
- Is the reported theft actually a misunderstanding, concealment, fraud, or family conflict?
- Which district does the case reveal?
- What consequence persists after resolution?
- Does it connect to the conspiracy? Current preference: probably not directly.
