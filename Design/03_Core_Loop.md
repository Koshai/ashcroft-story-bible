# Core Investigation Loop

**Status:** CANON

1. **Intake** — Understand why Daniel is involved, what is known, and which assumptions already exist.
2. **Observe** — Read the scene, environment, behavior, and context.
3. **Document** — Record information without prematurely assigning meaning.
4. **Organize** — Arrange people, events, locations, relationships, and timelines.
5. **Question** — Identify gaps, contradictions, and unsupported claims.
6. **Hypothesize** — Build multiple plausible explanations.
7. **Investigate** — Seek information capable of supporting or disproving each explanation.
8. **Challenge** — Pressure-test the favored theory against alternatives.
9. **Revise** — Abandon or modify theories when the evidence changes.
10. **Reach Reasonable Confidence** — Select the strongest explanation supported by verified information.
11. **Present** — Explain the conclusion and supporting reasoning.
12. **Reflect** — Experience legal, social, moral, and relationship consequences.

## Loop Principle

Being wrong is part of investigation. The game should describe why a theory is weak or incomplete without simply declaring the player unintelligent.
