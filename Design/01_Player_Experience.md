# Player Experience

**Status:** CANON

## Intended Experience

The player should move through a cycle of curiosity, confidence, doubt, revision, and earned understanding.

## What the Player Does

- Reads scenes rather than scanning for highlighted objects
- Documents observations
- Interviews people whose memories, motives, and knowledge differ
- Compares physical, digital, documentary, and testimonial information
- Builds timelines and relationship maps
- Forms more than one plausible hypothesis
- Attempts to disprove favored theories
- Presents findings at reasonable confidence
- Lives with consequences when conclusions are incomplete or wrong

## What the Player Does Not Do

- Gain detective statistics or RPG skill points
- Activate supernatural detective vision
- Click every hotspot until the answer unlocks
- Receive automatic deductions from Daniel
- Treat recurring characters as clue dispensers or upgrade vendors

## Emotional Rhythm

Investigations should alternate between:

- Focused tension
- Intellectual discovery
- Human consequence
- Quiet reflection

The city and relationships provide context and emotional weight, but the investigation remains the primary engagement.
