# Game Spine

**Status:** OPEN / PRE-PRODUCTION BASELINE

This document defines the whole-game structure before individual case production begins.

## Current Direction

The game will contain a finite sequence of substantial investigations arranged into acts. Cases are the primary playable units. Locations, citizens, organizations, and mechanics are introduced or expanded when cases require them.

## Proposed Macro Structure

| Segment | Player Experience | Investigation Emphasis |
|---|---|---|
| Prologue | Curiosity | Observation and documentation |
| Act I | Growing confidence | Interviews, timelines, contradictions |
| Act II | Doubt | Competing explanations and unreliable information |
| Act III | Obsession | Cross-case reasoning and larger patterns |
| Act IV | Responsibility | Institutional action, moral stakes, consequences |
| Finale | Conviction under uncertainty | Presenting and defending the complete theory |

## Case Count

**OPEN.** A working range of approximately 18–20 investigations has been discussed, but no number is canon until the case curriculum and production scope are tested.
