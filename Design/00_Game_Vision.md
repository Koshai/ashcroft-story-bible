# Game Vision

**Status:** CANON

## One-Sentence Vision

**Ashcroft City is a narrative investigation game in which the player learns to think like a detective by observing truthful information, organizing it, building competing explanations, challenging assumptions, and presenting the strongest conclusion supported by the available evidence.**

## Genre

- Narrative investigation game
- Grounded neo-noir crime drama
- Top-down 2D pixel-art presentation
- No supernatural elements
- Not an RPG

## Player Fantasy

The player should feel that they personally reached a conclusion through observation and reasoning—not that Daniel Mercer solved the case automatically or that the game handed them an answer.

## Product Identity

Ashcroft combines three connected experiences:

1. **The Case** — Solve an immediate human mystery.
2. **The People** — Understand the consequences, motives, and relationships surrounding it.
3. **The Pattern** — Notice connections that may span several investigations.

## North-Star Question

> Does this design make the player think more deeply like a detective?

If not, it must be revised, moved to the sandbox, or removed.
