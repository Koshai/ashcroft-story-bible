# Case Framework

**Status:** CANON

Cases are the primary units of gameplay, narrative progression, level design, and demand-driven worldbuilding.

## Three Case Layers

### The Case
The immediate incident and its resolution.

### The People
The motives, relationships, consequences, and moral stakes.

### The Pattern
Any cross-case connection or recurring system. Not every case contributes to the greater mystery.

## Case Requirements

Every case must provide:

- A coherent objective truth
- A reasonable initial interpretation
- Truthful but potentially misleading information
- More than one plausible hypothesis at some stage
- Contradictions the player can investigate
- A fair path to reasonable confidence
- Human consequences
- A defined investigative skill or reasoning emphasis
- A focused set of playable locations
- A list of world elements created, reused, or modified

## Demand-Driven Worldbuilding

A case may introduce or revise characters, locations, organizations, social structures, or culture when needed. Such additions must:

1. Serve the investigation or its consequences.
2. Fit existing canon or explicitly update it.
3. Remain useful beyond a single exposition scene when practical.
4. Avoid expanding scope without adding meaningful depth.
