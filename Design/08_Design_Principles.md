# Consolidated Design Principles

**Status:** CANON

1. **Investigation First** — World, narrative, and systems serve the detective experience.
2. **Player Performs the Deduction** — Daniel does not automatically solve the case.
3. **Reasonable Confidence** — Advancement requires the strongest supported theory, not perfect certainty.
4. **Truthful Fairness** — The game may misdirect through perspective and incomplete knowledge, but must provide a fair evidentiary path.
5. **No Detective Vision** — No magical highlighting or automatic hotspot solution.
6. **Perspective, Not Answers** — Recurring characters change the player's questions rather than reveal solutions.
7. **Demand-Driven Worldbuilding** — Add world elements when cases or consequences require them.
8. **Dual Purpose** — Important elements should serve both a narrative and investigative/gameplay function.
9. **People Before Plot** — Recurring people possess lives and goals independent of the current mystery.
10. **Depth Over Breadth** — Reuse and deepen familiar locations, citizens, and institutions.
11. **Failure Has Consequences** — Wrong or incomplete conclusions continue the narrative where feasible.
12. **Ordinary First** — Institutions and places must have credible everyday purposes before mystery relevance.
13. **Quiet Moments Matter** — Reflection creates meaning and changes how Daniel approaches investigations.
14. **The World Is Living Canon** — Non-constitutional details may be revised when the game becomes stronger.
