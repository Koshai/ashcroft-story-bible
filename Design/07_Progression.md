# Progression Philosophy

**Status:** CANON

Ashcroft does not use conventional RPG progression.

## Player Progression

The player's progression is cognitive:

- Asking better questions
- Distinguishing information from evidence
- Managing competing hypotheses
- Recognizing bias
- Reading timelines and relationships
- Connecting information across cases
- Accepting and revising wrong theories

## Daniel's Progression

Daniel changes through:

- Relationships
- Consequences
- Mistakes
- Reflection
- Professional conflict
- Moral responsibility

He does not become more capable through numerical investigation stats.

## Mechanical Progression

New tools or interfaces may be introduced when later cases demand them, but they must expand the forms of reasoning available rather than function as arbitrary upgrades.
