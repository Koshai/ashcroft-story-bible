# Level Design Philosophy

**Status:** CANON FOUNDATION

Ashcroft is not designed as a giant open world. Playable levels are focused investigative spaces connected by narrative travel and recurring city locations.

## Level Unit

A case may contain several locations, such as:

- Crime or incident scene
- Client or witness location
- Public records or institutional location
- Surveillance area
- Daniel's office and whiteboard
- Personal reflection location

## Level Design Goals

Each location must support at least one meaningful activity:

- Observation
- Interview
- Reconstruction
- Documentary or digital analysis
- Surveillance or following
- Theory testing
- Consequence or reflection

## Reuse Principle

Returning to a known location should reveal change:

- Different people or departments
- New social context
- Consequences from earlier cases
- Changed access or relationships
- New meaning attached to familiar details

Familiarity should deepen the city while controlling production scope.
