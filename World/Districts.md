# Districts of Ashcroft

**Status:** Names and broad identities are CANON; boundaries and detailed content remain Living Canon.

## Historic Quarter — LOC-003

Original city center, aging architecture, civic memory, old businesses, cultural institutions, and Daniel's small office nearby.

## Civic Center — LOC-004

City Hall, courts, APD administration, public records, and government agencies.

## Financial District — LOC-005

Banks, investment firms, corporate offices, law firms, and concentrated visible power.

## University District — LOC-006

Ashcroft University, research facilities, student housing, laboratories, and institutional influence.

## Ashcroft Heights — LOC-007

Old wealth, influential families, private schools, mansions, and exclusive institutions.

## Northwood Park — LOC-008

Middle-class residential communities. Daniel currently lives here.

## Warehouse District — LOC-009

Active logistics operations mixed with abandoned warehouses and redevelopment pressure.

## Old Harbor — LOC-010

Historic waterfront, aging piers, fishing culture, and the city's industrial memory.

## Riverfront — LOC-011

Luxury residences, nightlife, tourism, entertainment, and polished redevelopment.

## South Docks — LOC-012

Modern international cargo operations and major port infrastructure.

## Industrial Belt — LOC-013

Factories, freight lines, utilities, storage facilities, and abandoned industrial sites.

## Eastside Flats — LOC-014

Working-class district heavily affected by industrial decline. Childhood home of Daniel and Becca; current home of Becca and Elaine; location of Mike's garage.
