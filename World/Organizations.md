# Organizations and Power Structure

## Ecosystem Model

Ashcroft's institutions are organized through six ecosystems documented in [`../Ecosystems/README.md`](../Ecosystems/README.md): Government, Justice, Health, Commerce, Information, and Community.

## Visible Power

- Mayor and civic administration
- Police leadership
- District Attorney and judges
- Corporate executives
- Media leadership
- University and hospital leadership

## Practical Power

- Developers and property owners
- Investment firms and banks
- Shipping and logistics companies
- Insurers
- Lobbyists
- Private security interests
- Professional networks and long-standing relationships

## Hidden Power

The hidden layer is not a formal society with universal membership. It is an evolved network of overlapping interests. Participants may understand only their immediate role, cooperate without knowing the complete consequences, or compete against other parts of the same network.

The network survives by exploiting ordinary institutional fragmentation, not by controlling every institution.

## Addition Rule

Before adding an organization, identify:

1. Its primary ecosystem.
2. The city function it performs without Daniel.
3. Existing organizations that depend on it.
4. How it deepens the current world.
5. Whether an existing organization can serve the same purpose.
