# Conspiracy Factions

**Status:** OPEN

## Structural Rule

The hidden network has no required universal membership, single command structure, or shared ideology. "Faction" refers to a cluster of aligned interests, not necessarily a named organization.

Future factions may emerge from:

- Real estate and redevelopment
- Shipping and port logistics
- Finance and investment
- City government
- Universities and medical research
- Media
- Private security
- Historic families
- Organized crime

Law-enforcement individuals may become involved, but the APD as an institution is not a conspiracy faction.

## Faction Requirements

Each faction must have:

- A concrete interest
- A plausible public justification
- Internal disagreement
- Different knowledge levels
- Something it fears losing
- A dependency on at least one other ecosystem
- Actions that remain understandable at the local level while contributing to a larger pattern

## Scope Rule

Do not create factions merely to make the network larger. Prefer deepening existing economic, civic, and interpersonal relationships.
