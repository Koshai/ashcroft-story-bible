# Conspiracy Factions

**Status:** OPEN

The hidden network should not have formal universal membership. Future factions may emerge from:

- Real estate and redevelopment
- Shipping and port logistics
- Finance and investment
- City government
- Law enforcement
- Universities and medical research
- Media
- Private security
- Historic families
- Organized crime

Each faction must have:

- A concrete interest
- A public justification
- Internal disagreement
- Different knowledge levels
- Something it fears losing
