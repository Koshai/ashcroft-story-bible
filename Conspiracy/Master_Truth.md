# Main Conspiracy — Master Truth

**Status:** OPEN — exact purpose, actors, history, and outcome remain unresolved

## Canonized Form

The greater mystery is a distributed network, not a single formal secret society. There is no requirement for one mastermind, headquarters, membership list, or universally shared objective.

Participants may:

- Know only their immediate transaction or obligation
- Commit relatively small crimes that combine into a larger structure
- Cooperate for different reasons
- Compete with other parts of the network
- Believe they are protecting Ashcroft or their own institution
- Be unaware that their actions contribute to a larger pattern

## Central Investigative Question

The mystery asks:

> Why did no one see the whole pattern?

The antagonist is partly fragmentation. Each institution sees the portion assigned to it, and many individual cases may be competently resolved without exposing their shared structure.

## Relationship to APD

APD is not the conspiracy. The network benefits from APD and other institutions functioning normally within bounded responsibilities.

The APD may solve a homicide, Financial Crimes may resolve a fraud, Health may classify a death, and Government may process a permit. None of those actions must be corrupt or incorrect for the cross-case pattern to remain hidden.

Daniel's defining advantage is his refusal to accept that investigative boundaries describe reality.

Daniel uncovers and demonstrates the network. APD, prosecutors, courts, journalists, and other civic actors must perform their own legitimate roles in exposing and addressing it.

## Emergent-Conspiracy Rule

Standalone cases must remain satisfying on their own. Connections should accumulate gradually among recurring people, businesses, records, places, and institutions.

The greater pattern should feel inevitable in hindsight, not artificially attached to every case.

Not every crime belongs to the network.

## Established Requirements

The conspiracy must:

- Have historical roots
- Precede Daniel's involvement
- Involve multiple interests rather than one cartoonish society
- Include participants with different levels of knowledge
- Contain internal conflict and competing goals
- Connect some, but not all, cases
- Evolve over time
- Reflect Ashcroft's history of progress, displacement, and institutional compromise
- Exploit cross-ecosystem dependencies and institutional fragmentation
- Require collective civic action for meaningful resolution
