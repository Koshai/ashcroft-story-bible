# SYS-006 — Relationship Consequence System

## Principle

Relationships are not side content or affection meters. They respond to Daniel's actions across cases.

## Tracked Dimensions — Conceptual

- Trust
- Honesty
- Availability
- Respect
- Emotional safety
- Boundaries
- Mutual support
- Resentment

These do not necessarily need to appear as visible numerical meters.

## Case Interaction

Choices in investigations may affect relationships through:

- Cancelled plans
- Confidentiality
- Lies or omissions
- Reckless risk
- Wrong accusations
- Moral compromises
- Emotional withdrawal
- Whether Daniel listens to warnings

## Bidirectional Influence

Relationships can support or destabilize Daniel, but should not create arbitrary stat bonuses or penalties. Changes in his state of mind should be expressed through scenes, available dialogue, patience, assumptions, and narrative behavior.
