# SYS-005 — Roots and Reflection

## Core Principle

The people closest to Daniel do not solve his cases. They shape the detective capable of solving them.

## Sources of Perspective

### Thomas — Objective Truth

Question: What can I prove?

### Elaine — History and Context

Question: Who was this person before the event?

### Becca — Consequence and Compassion

Question: Who is affected if I am right?

### Mike — Alternative Paths

Question: What if my first assumption is reversed?

### Romantic Partner — Reflection and Trust

Question: Who am I becoming, and can I still trust and be trusted?

## Recurring Use

Daniel can return to familiar people and places throughout the game. These moments provide emotional recalibration and may lead the player to reconsider a case from another angle.

## The Home Chapter

### Working Title

**Home**

### Function

A dedicated chapter or sequence returns Daniel to the Mercer home and his roots during a personal or professional low point.

Possible activities:

- Clean Thomas's workshop
- Review old photographs
- Read handwritten notes
- Find school reports and childhood drawings
- Hear conflicting family memories
- Participate in an ordinary meal

### Rule

The chapter does not reveal a hidden conspiracy clue.

### Lasting Effect

Daniel emerges with a more holistic approach:

- Critical thinking without tunnel vision
- Moral and ethical awareness
- Willingness to reverse assumptions
- Sensitivity to missing context
- Renewed motivation
- Recognition that evidence and people must be understood together
