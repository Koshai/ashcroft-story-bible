# SYS-004 — Knowledge Tracking

Every mystery should maintain three separate layers:

## Objective Truth

What actually occurred, independent of what anyone believes.

## Character Knowledge

For every relevant character:

- What they know
- What they believe
- What they misunderstand
- What they conceal
- Why they conceal it

## Player Knowledge

- Evidence discovered
- Hypotheses formed
- Conclusions reached
- Mistakes made
- Information permanently missed

This separation prevents characters from acting on information they should not possess and allows failure to remain coherent.
