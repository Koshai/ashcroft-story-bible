# SYS-001 — Detective System

## Core Loop

1. Receive or discover a case
2. Inspect scenes
3. Gather physical, digital, documentary, and testimonial evidence
4. Interview witnesses and suspects
5. Construct timelines
6. Identify contradictions
7. Form hypotheses
8. Test competing explanations
9. Reach a conclusion or decide uncertainty remains
10. Experience consequences

## Player Fantasy

The player should feel they genuinely reasoned toward a conclusion rather than selected the answer the game expected.

## Growth Philosophy

Daniel unlocks better thinking rather than merely better equipment.

Possible learned principles:

- Evidence without context can mislead.
- A motive is rarely only one thing.
- Ask who benefits and who pays.
- Consider who the person was before the event.
- Reverse the original assumption.
- Distinguish what happened from why it happened.
- Consider the consequences of being right.
