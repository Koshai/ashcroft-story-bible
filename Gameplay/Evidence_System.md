# SYS-002 — Evidence and Contradiction System

## Evidence Types

- Physical objects
- Documents
- Financial records
- Phone and digital records
- CCTV and photographs
- Testimony
- Environmental conditions
- Absences and missing expected evidence

## Contradictions

Daniel's defining ability is noticing when pieces do not fit. The system should support:

- Conflicting statements
- Impossible timelines
- Mismatched records
- Missing expected behavior
- Alternative interpretations of the same evidence
- Deliberate lies and sincere mistakes

## Principle

Objects do not interpret themselves. Physical evidence may be accurate while Daniel's assumptions about its meaning are wrong.
