# SYS-007 — Failure and Consequences

## Core Rule

The player may fail without the story ending.

## Forms of Failure

- Incorrect accusation
- Incomplete conclusion
- Missed evidence
- Broken trust
- Lost client
- Public embarrassment
- Institutional retaliation
- Harm to an innocent person
- A guilty person remaining free

## Canonical Consequence

Mistakes become part of Daniel's history and may affect later cases, relationships, reputation, and self-perception.

## Design Warning

Failure must remain understandable. The player should be able to trace consequences back to evidence, assumptions, choices, or uncertainty—not hidden arbitrary triggers.
